Yo, new version of the studio kit is attached (version 2). Lots of upgrades: haul + vertical Short video types, a render queue, captions, a smarter music picker that uses your whole music folder by mood, new intro templates with a quality check, cleaner cuts on reviews, zooms on screen recordings, a privacy blur scan, and a new look.

You DON'T have to set it up again. To update:
1. Close the Studio.
2. Unzip "Creator Studio Kit.zip" into your Downloads (NOT over your current kit).
3. Open the new folder and double-click UPDATE.cmd. It finds your kit, backs it up and updates it. Your settings, channels, codes and projects stay.
4. Open Claude Code in your normal kit folder and paste what's in UPDATE PROMPT.md. Claude finishes it off and test-builds a video.
5. Delete the folder in Downloads.

Every update from now on works the same way.
