# Setup Questions

Claude asks you these during setup, a few at a time with click-buttons. You can also answer them here first and
paste your answers in. Skip anything you're not sure about: there are sensible defaults, and you can change any
answer later. Change the answers file, run `python configure.py` again, and the studio updates.

The Studio's setup window (it opens the first time the Studio starts; Settings → Setup runs it again) already asks
the folders (13–14), captions (16), the music search (18) and your channel name, so Claude skips those.

## A. You and your channels
1. What should your studio app be called? (It shows in the window title.)
2. List every channel you make videos for. For each one: its name, the type of video (phone/app tutorial, product review,
   gaming, screen recording/walkthrough, talking/podcast, other), and how many uploads per week you're aiming for.
3. How does each channel's intro start? (e.g. "Hey guys, ___ here", "Hello YouTube"). The studio recognises the channel from this.
4. Who are 3–5 creators whose editing you love? (Their videos become your style references.)

## B. Your recordings
5. What do you record with? (OBS, phone, camera, screen recorder, Tella, Gling, CapCut…)
6. What does a raw recording look like? Webcam + screen side by side (one wide 3840×1080 file)? Separate camera + screen files?
   Screen only (no face)? Phone vertical? One camera or two?
7. Microphones: one mic, or a main mic + a clip-on (DJI etc.)? Are they on separate audio tracks?
8. Do you show a phone on screen (phone mirroring)? Does your home address, real name, phone number or email ever show up?
   (If yes, they get blurred automatically. You type them into a private file that never leaves your PC.)
9. Do you record the intro voiceover separately? Where do those audio files end up?
10. Do you already cut talking with Gling (or similar) and export XML, or should the studio do all the cutting?
11. How long are typical raw recordings, and how long are the finished videos?

## C. Your PC
12. Do you have an NVIDIA graphics card? (Check: Task Manager → Performance → GPU.) If not, the studio switches to CPU encoding, which is slower but works.
13. Which drive has the most free space for projects? (Raw footage needs ~10–30 GB per video while editing.)
14. Where do you want: video projects, your music library, and finished videos?

## D. Style: your eye ([taste] rules in the Playbook)
15. Filler words: cut lightly (um/uh only) or heavily (also "and yeah", "so yeah", lone "alright"/"ya")?
16. Captions on long videos: none (keyword graphics instead), or burned-in subtitles?
17. Face cam: full webcam with the screen as picture-in-picture, a corner cam over the screen, or no cam?
18. Music: type beats, lo-fi, game soundtracks, cinematic, or none? Shall I look for the music already on your PC and sort it
    into a mood library for you? (Recommended. Your files stay where they are; tracks with vocals or producer tags are left out.)
    Is there a folder I should also look in, or one I should skip?
19. Sound effects: minimal (soft swooshes on transitions), medium, or high-energy?
20. Intros: motion-graphic intros from your voiceover (recommended), a short title card, or no intro?
21. Pacing: relaxed (natural pauses) or tight (every pause trimmed between sentences)?
22. Anything you NEVER want to see in your videos?

## E. After the edit
23. Do you want Shorts cut automatically from every long video?
24. Do you want Windows pop-up notifications when a draft or final is ready?
25. Do you use any AI generation tools (Higgsfield, Runway, ElevenLabs…)? Should Claude ever spend credits without asking? (Default: never.)

## F. Learning
26. What do you most want to get better at: cutting, sound, intros/motion graphics, storytelling, or speed?
27. How much do you want explained? (a) just do it, (b) a one-line "why" with each change, (c) teach me as we go.
28. How many videos a week can you realistically record right now?
