# Intro style catalog (80 styles, 20 per type)

Built 2026-10-25. Rules: `INTRO_RULES.md`. Data: `_styles/styles.json`. Gallery with his verdicts: https://claude.ai/artifact/MZm9akvvCr7k8NvhW7inU9

A style is an editorial DEVICE layered over the type's approved text layer. Tier: safe = built from approved patterns, fresh = new but inside the rules, bold = test render first.

## Minecraft

Text layer: KV Pixel Box text (calm baseline) on top of the device. Game OST music, drop on the turn line, XP bar on 'watch to the very end', 'Let's Go!' end.

### M01 Main Menu Director (safe)
The intro happens inside the real Minecraft menus. Every line is a button he 'clicks' that opens the literal thing.

- Fits: servers, realms, marketplace, settings / how-to, console players
- Assets: Real game UI, Gameplay / Director B-roll, Official logos
- Beats: hook: Title screen pans in, cursor hovers 'Play' | problem: 'Can't add servers on console' = the real Add Server button, red lock, shake | turn: 'In today's video' = 'Servers' tab clicked, list slides in | payoff: Last click 'Join Server' = gameplay montage, 'Let's Go!'
- Signature: The cursor click on the exact word, using his own recorded menus (Top 10 Servers raw has the Servers tab at 0:44-1:04).
- Footage: His raw recording's menu stretches; a fresh menu capture if missing. Never a drawn fake menu when a real one exists.
- Motion: Slow push toward each button, a push-glide into the screen it opens. Sound: Soft UI click on each press, nothing else. Music drop on the 'Servers' click.
- Guard: Readable IPs blurred. No more than 3 menu screens before a gameplay shot.

### M02 Loading Screen Spine (safe)
The real 'Generating world / Loading resources' bar is the spine: each line fills it a bit more; it finishes on the turn and the world opens.

- Fits: any top-N, add-ons, realms, new world / survival
- Assets: Real game UI, Gameplay / Director B-roll
- Beats: hook: Black-free start on the loading screen with the dirt background | problem: Problem lines play as small tips text under the bar ('most servers never get seen') | turn: Bar hits 100% on 'in today's video', the world fades up full frame | payoff: Rank montage, 'Let's Go!'
- Signature: Matches the KV body's loading-screen rank cards, so intro and body feel like one edit.
- Footage: Real loading screen captured from his game + his gameplay.
- Motion: Camera creeps toward the bar; on 100% a smooth push through the screen into the world. Sound: Quiet tick as the bar steps, music opens on 100%.
- Guard: Never a dip to black between loading screen and world. Bar must not stall longer than 3.5 s without a cutaway.

### M03 Payoff First (safe)
Open on the single best moment of the whole video (2-3 s, the #1's coolest shot or the funniest fail), freeze, then rewind into the VO's problem.

- Fits: any video with a standout clip, dragon / boss add-ons, PvP servers, funny moments
- Assets: Gameplay / Director B-roll, Real game UI
- Beats: hook: Best moment plays full frame, freeze on its peak | problem: Rewind scrub (fast reverse) into the problem lines | turn: Turn on 'in today's video' with the drop | payoff: Payoff montage without spoiling the #1 name
- Signature: The freeze frame on the peak; the viewer has seen the promise before a word is said.
- Footage: Picked from the edited body (the moment with the biggest motion score + his loudest reaction).
- Motion: Hard real-time play, freeze, smooth 0.6 s reverse scrub (no glitch). Sound: The clip's own game audio for 1 s, then the VO; a tape-stop is too corny, use a clean freeze.
- Guard: The freeze shows the moment, not the #1 name (top-N blur rule).

### M04 Search & Dive (safe)
He searches the topic (YouTube / MCPEDL / CurseForge), the results page scrolls full screen, the cursor picks a result and the camera dives into it.

- Fits: add-ons, shaders, clients, 'everyone is searching for'
- Assets: Fresh YouTube search, Named site page, Gameplay / Director B-roll, YouTube thumbnail wall
- Beats: hook: Query typed in the real search bar | problem: Results scroll full screen, slow-fast-slow with motion blur | turn: Gold frame + click on the result the line is about | payoff: Dive into gameplay; repeat once max, with a different site
- Signature: The approved Jordan Dueck website scroll effect, recorded fresh for this video (a reused recording is 'a dead giveaway').
- Footage: Live screen recording of a NEW query each video (filter this month).
- Motion: Full-screen page, vertical motion blur on the fast part, punch-in on the target. Sound: One whoosh on the fast scroll, click on the pick.
- Guard: Max 2 page scrolls in the intro, never back to back. Never his own link page.

### M05 Spectator Fly-Through (fresh)
One continuous spectator-mode camera flight through the worlds of the video; KV phrases appear as the camera passes. One moving world, no cuts until the turn.

- Fits: realms, SMP / survival servers, builds, texture packs, shaders
- Assets: Gameplay / Director B-roll, Real game UI
- Beats: hook: Low glide over the ground at dawn | problem: Problem lines: the camera rises, the world below looks empty | turn: Turn: camera flips up through the clouds and drops into entry #1's lobby (blurred name) | payoff: Fast flight montage through every entry, 'Let's Go!'
- Signature: The whole intro feels like one camera move; the measured trait of the Minecraft intros he loves (motion 2-8 every second).
- Footage: Director add-on in-game cinematic paths (reel-code flash + brollmap) recorded in each server/realm.
- Motion: Continuous; KV boxes ride the world in light parallax. Sound: Only music + VO + one soft swoosh per phrase.
- Guard: Needs the Director recording on filming day: add it to the shot list before he records.

## Phone / app

Text layer: P21 glowing 2-4 word phrase captions, one track, low. One steady type beat. No flashes, push/glide transitions, story SFX on his words.
